THE IDENTITY GAP

Authentication verifies access.
TrustMesh investigates continuity.

A person can pass an initial login check while an account may later be used by someone else. Static authentication alone cannot establish continuous identity throughout an entire session. At the same time, legitimate drivers change devices, travel to unfamiliar locations, and adapt their routines. The real challenge isn't detecting every change—it's distinguishing meaningful changes from normal ones.

Static Verification

A valid credential or one-time biometric login establishes entry permission, but cannot prove who continues operating the vehicle or account 45 minutes later.

Behavioral Changes

Potential account handoffs create subtle discontinuities across device acceleration, route navigation cadence, and interaction telemetry that static checks completely miss.

False Alarms

Legitimate changes—such as new phones, detours, or borrowing a spouse's car—should not automatically be treated as identity misuse or trigger punitive account blocks.

The Continuous Identity Vulnerability Window

Standard login protocols verify identity only at Minute 0. TrustMesh maintains non-intrusive contextual vigilance throughout the entire session timeline.

CORE ARCHITECTURE

Dual-axis trust assessment

Evaluate contextual continuity and behavioral continuity separately, helping the system distinguish different types of deviations without confusing a new phone with a driver handoff.

TEMPORAL MODELING

Dual-timescale baselines

Compare activity against longer-term habits and more recent patterns to better interpret gradual changes, seasonal shifts, and sudden handoff deviations.

IDENTITY DYNAMICS

Account-handoff awareness

Analyze complex scenarios where account usage shifts between different individuals and later returns to the original account holder, isolating shared fleets.

TRANSPARENCY

Explainable risk signals

Present the concrete factors contributing to an alert instead of relying on an unexplained black-box risk score, empowering rapid, defensible admin auditability.

FRICTION REDUCTION

Legitimate-change awareness

Incorporate declared device changes, planned travel, and contextual notes when interpreting unusual activity to safeguard honest driver productivity.

ETHICAL GOVERNANCE

Privacy-conscious design

Emphasize proportionate signal collection, strictly minimized data exposure, and clear opportunities for driver review, verification, and appeal.
